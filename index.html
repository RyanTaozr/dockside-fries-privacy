Dockside Fries — Privacy Policy

Developer: ZRTao
Effective date: October 2, 2026
Applies to the offline Android test releases without advertising, accounts, or analytics.

Information handled by this version

This version does not create user accounts and does not send personal information or gameplay data to the developer. It contains no advertising SDK, in-app purchases, or developer analytics.

Local game progress

The game stores progress and settings in the app's local storage on your device. This includes earned game currency, unlocked characters and upgrades, and gameplay preferences. These records are used to resume your game and are not uploaded by the app. This version does not provide cloud synchronization.

Deleting local records

You can delete the app's stored records through Android Settings by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling it. This erases saved progress.

Google Play and support

Google Play and Android may separately process information when you download, update, or use your device, under Google's own policies. If you contact the developer, information you voluntarily include in your message will be used to respond to your request.

Contact and future changes

Contact ZRTao at ryan.taozr@gmail.com. If a future release adds advertising, purchases, accounts, or analytics, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated to describe the resulting data processing.
